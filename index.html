About me

I am a lecturer and researcher in transport engineering at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia. My work combines field measurements, statistics and digital tools to understand how Phnom Penh's streets work for the people who use them, and to show how they could work better.

Evidence-based. Practical. Built for Cambodia.

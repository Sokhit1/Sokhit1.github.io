Safer streets. Cleaner air. Better ways to move around Phnom Penh.

See my research Google Scholar

Most trips in Phnom Penh are made on foot or by motorcycle. I measure what that means for the people walking and the air they breathe, and turn it into evidence for better streets.

Research

Four areas, one city.

Walkability

Walkability indices weighted by what local pedestrians value, and the quality of new sidewalks.

Traffic and air quality

How traffic mix shapes pedestrian exposure to fine particles, and what motorcycle emissions add.

Public transport

Route planning for Phnom Penh's city buses.

Air transport and digital twins

Airport capacity, airline planning and sustainable aviation, plus 3D digital twins of logistics parks and school zones.

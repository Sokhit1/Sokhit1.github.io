Dr. PLACK Sokhit

Lecturer in Transport Engineering at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia. I study how people walk, breathe and travel in Phnom Penh, and build tools that help plan safer, cleaner transport.

Read my publications Google Scholar GitHub

Research

Phnom Penh is growing fast, and most trips are made on foot or by motorcycle. My work measures what that means for pedestrians and air quality, and turns the results into evidence for street design and transport policy.

Walkability and pedestrians

Walkability indices weighted by what local pedestrians actually value, and the quality and use of new sidewalks in Phnom Penh.

Traffic and air quality

How traffic composition shapes pedestrian exposure to fine particles, and what motorcycle emissions contribute at street and parking level.

Public transport

Network analysis and route planning for Phnom Penh's city bus system.

Air transport and digital twins

Airport capacity, airline planning and sustainable aviation, and 3D digital twins of logistics parks and school zones linked to live data.
